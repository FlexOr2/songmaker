Picture owner · issue #229

Album cover suggestions

A static decision picture for the album-level cover flow. It uses CSS-only placeholder art: the product chooses from three generated suggestions, then carries the selected album cover into the rail and share page.

Ruled expectations, located in the picture

01

Create. “Suggest cover” is deliberate; it produces three choices from album title, style and a lyrics excerpt. No cover is generated automatically.

02

Change / remove. The chosen cover can be replaced with a new suggestion or upload, and removed back to the initial.

03

Identity. The cover belongs to the album, not a song; its rail tile and share page use the same image.

04

States. Generating shows progress. Failure names the reason — never a silent fallback.

05

Secrets and rights. Generation uses the operator’s subscribed CLI; only the album owner or an admin may start it; prompts contain album metadata only. Awaiting spoken ruling.

06

Limits. Exactly three suggestions per click; at most 10 generations per album/day (configured); 1024×1024 PNG stored in the audio volume.
