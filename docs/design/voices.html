Picture owner · issue #232 · S5/S6

Voices and a model-bound picker

A static decision picture for training a private voice from a musician’s own recordings, then choosing it only with its compatible model. Each frame is an honest state, not a promise hidden behind a button.

Ruled expectations, located in the picture

01

Create. A musician creates a voice from their own takes or uploads, adds samples, then starts training. Foreign takes are never selectable. Located in (a).

02

States. Training shows epochs and time remaining; waiting names the occupied worker and queue position; failure names its reason; cancellation is explicit. Located in (b), (d).

03

Identity. Every ready voice carries its training mode, sft or turbo. The picker offers it only to a matching model, otherwise “not available for this model”. Located in (c), (e).

04

Delete / undo. Deleting a used voice is confirmed. Existing takes remain playable, and retain the explicit “voice deleted” label rather than silently changing voice. Located in (e), (f).

05

Rights. Voices are private to the musician. An admin can see their operational state but cannot select or use them. Located in (h).

06

Limits. Configuration allows at most N = 10 voices per musician and M = 2 queued trainings. Rejection says which limit was reached. Located in (g).
