## Song Concept

A melodic house reimagining of Liquido's "Narcotic" — but the drug isn't a person, it's **being young in a small town with your best friend.** Summer nights on bicycles, sneaking out at midnight, the smell of warm asphalt and cheap beer, talking until the sun came up about everything and nothing. Two kids who thought the world was a secret only they knew. The original song's restless "I can't quit you" energy becomes nostalgia you can't shake — the high of a decade that ended before you noticed.

**Core thesis**: Youth was the narcotic. The comedown is called growing up.

**Emotional arc**: Quiet remembering → the rush hits → full euphoria → the ache of distance → peace

**Dedication**: Flex0r for MC Tobbisch — Erlangen summers, still warm.

### Key Design Decisions
- **"Narcotic" = youth itself**: Not about a person, not about gaming — the drug is being young with your best friend in a small town. The high you didn't know you were on until it was gone.
- **Circular structure**: Intro and outro use the same whispered line — "Close your eyes / We're back again" — the memory loop never breaks.
- **Physical specificity**: Late-night phone calls, yellow traffic lights, kitchen floors at 4 AM, satellites mistaken for stars, the bridge you almost jumped off. Every line is a photograph.
- **Only two names**: Flex0r and Tobbisch. This isn't a squad anthem — it's a letter from one friend to another.
- **Erlangen appears once**: In the bridge, whispered. The city doesn't need to be named twice — you feel it in every line before that.
- **Bridge as the gut punch**: Drops the poetry for direct address — "Tobbisch, we're older now." Raw, no metaphor, just truth.
- **Final chorus shift**: "stolen plans" becomes "no-name plans", "ran free" becomes "set us free" — the past tense shifts to present truth.
- **"We'll never crash"**: The narcotic metaphor's payoff — the high of this friendship doesn't end.
- **Bark-optimized**: Short English phrases (max 8 words per line), repeated "Narcotic nights" hook, clear consonants for Bark to deliver cleanly.
