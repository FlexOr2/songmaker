## Song Concept

One physical sensation: **standing completely still while the entire world moves around you.** Not meditation — defiance. The city spins, the traffic blurs, the crowd pushes past, and you just... stop. The verses are motion — running, chasing, sprinting through years. The choruses are the sudden freeze. The bridge is the revelation: stillness isn't giving up. It's the first honest thing you've done.

**Core thesis**: Speed is the lie. Stillness is the rebellion.

**Emotional arc**: Rushing → exhaustion → sudden stop → clarity → peace

### Key Design Decisions
- **"Stand Still" = paradox**: A melodic house track about NOT moving. The music pushes forward, the lyric resists. Tension drives the whole thing.
- **6-word chorus hook**: "Let the whole world blur" — visual, immediate, singable.
- **Physical imagery only**: Doors, clocks, crowns, traffic, stones, water, phone, wind, streetlights. You SEE every line. No abstractions.
- **Contrast with Track 01**: "Let Me Fall" is about releasing downward. "Stand Still" is about refusing to move at all. Both are about stopping the fight with a different axis.
- **Bridge payoff**: "Here is enough" — the simplest possible line after 3 minutes of running imagery. Earned simplicity.
- **Final chorus variation**: "I stopped running from my life" replaces "I've been running all my life" — past tense shift marks the transformation.
- **Bark-optimized**: Short phrases (max 8 words per line), clear consonants, repeated "stand still" hook is easy for Bark to lock onto.
