## Song Concept

One physical sensation: **freefall as surrender.** Not falling as tragedy —
falling as the moment you stop white-knuckling your life and discover the drop
IS the destination. Every verse is the grip tightening. Every chorus is letting go.
The bridge reveals the truth: there is no bottom. Falling feels like flying.

**Core thesis**: Control is the prison. Falling is the freedom.

**Emotional arc**: Suffocation → grip slipping → freefall → euphoria → transcendence

### Key Design Decisions
- **"Fall" = "Drop"**: The word literally describes what the music does. The lyric IS the production.
- **8-word chorus**: "Let me fall, I don't need the ground" — singable after one listen.
- **Physical imagery only**: No abstract emotions. Walls, hands, air, windows, birds, cities, breathing lights. You SEE every line.
- **Pre-choruses earn the drop**: "Not scared anymore" (PC1) and "never meant to land" (PC2) — each one reframes falling.
- **Bridge payoff**: "Falling feels like flying" — earned by 3 minutes of physical specificity. Simple line that hits hard because of context.
- **Final chorus variation**: "standing still so long" replaces "holding on so long" — the final realization is that the opposite of falling isn't rising, it's standing still.
- **Bark-optimized**: Short phrases (max 8 words per line), repeated structure, clear consonants. Every line is built for Bark to deliver cleanly.
