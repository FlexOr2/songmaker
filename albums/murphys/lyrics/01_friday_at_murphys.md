# Friday at Murphy's

## Concept

The opener and main anthem. Friday night chaos at Murphy's — arriving around nine or ten when it's already packed, the crew behind the bar holding it all together, the regulars in their spots. Not focused on any one person — it's about the whole scene, the energy, the noise, the collective madness.
