# Saturday Morning

## Concept

The morning after Friday at Murphy's. Felix wakes up on the couch, still in his jacket, head pounding. Phone full of photos he doesn't remember taking. Kebab wrapper on the coffee table. A Murphy's coaster and someone's lighter in his pocket. Peter texts at 8am saying "best night ever" — Felix can't remember anything after eleven. He swears never again. He knows he'll be back next Friday. He always is.
