# Murphy's Law

## Concept

Murphy's Law: anything that can go wrong, will go wrong. At Murphy's pub, everything goes wrong — the tap sticks, Tom spills secrets, the pizza burns, the toilet's broken. But that's exactly what makes it perfect. Nobody leaves. Nobody cares. The chaos IS the charm. An Irish folk punk anthem about loving a beautifully broken pub. Double meaning: the pub Murphy's + Murphy's Law.
