# Murphy's Law

## Concept

Felix tells the story of THE St. Patrick's Day at Murphy's — the one where everything went wrong. Told in first person, Peter is his co-pilot in disaster. Peter's five Guinness deep when Felix arrives, immediately spills a pint down his shirt. The Guinness tap dies (Peter's devastated, Felix is fine — he drinks Weizen). Peter's pizza arrives burned. Peter bumps into a tourist, rips down the decorations. Power goes out, Maria pours from memory, the whole pub sings Danny Boy by phone light. Peter knocks over a table, Gitty's beer hits the floor again. Nobody leaves. Best night ever. The song never says "Murphy's Law" — it shows it.
