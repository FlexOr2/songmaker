# Whiskey O'Clock

## Concept

The slowest, darkest track on the album. After 11pm, the crowd is gone. Just Felix, a whiskey, and the signs on the wall. The real signs from Murphy's: "In God we trust, all others pay cash", "Ein Tag hat 24 Stunden, ein Kasten 24 Flaschen", "Borrow money from a pessimist." Slow blues, heavy guitar, Denglish because that's how you think at midnight after whiskey.
