# Marias Schicht

## Concept

German rap version of Maria's perspective. She runs Murphy's — pours the drinks, handles the crowd, closes up at 2am. Not sweet, not grateful — confident and real. The girls behind the bar are the ones keeping everything together while everyone else gets drunk and philosophical. Mix of German with some English thrown in naturally.
