# Last Call

## Concept

It's 2am. The bar is almost empty. Maria's wiping down the counter, chairs are going up on tables. Felix is the last one again — Peter left an hour ago, the regulars are gone. Just him, the last whiskey, and the walk home through Erlangen. Not sad — just that honest quiet moment when the night is over and you're not ready to go back to the empty apartment yet.
