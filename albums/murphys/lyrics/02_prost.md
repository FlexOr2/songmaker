# Prost!

## Concept

The drinking anthem. Pure fun, zero depth. Raise your glass, scream Prost, repeat. A Deutschpunk party song that could be played at Murphy's and everyone — staff, regulars, tourists — would scream along. References the real drinks at Murphy's: Sebaldus Weizen, Guinness, Jever, whiskey. No sad feelings, no philosophy, just beer and noise.
