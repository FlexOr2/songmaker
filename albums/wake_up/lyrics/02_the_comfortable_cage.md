# The Comfortable Cage

## Concept

Bitter Sweet Symphony for 2026. The cage isn't made of bars — it's made of convenience. Same-day delivery, infinite streaming, dopamine feeds curated just for you. You know something's wrong but the couch is warm and the algorithm knows exactly what to play next. You're not oppressed, you're optimized. Not enslaved, just subscribed. The most dangerous prison is the one you'd never leave. Sweeping orchestral rock, walking through the city, resigned but aware.
