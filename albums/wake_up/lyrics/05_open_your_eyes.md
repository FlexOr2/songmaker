# Open Your Eyes

## Concept
The turning point of the album. You've been sleepwalking, trapped in the
comfortable cage, running the rat race — and suddenly something breaks through.
Not a revolution, not an epiphany. Just a Tuesday morning where the sunrise
catches you off guard and you realize you haven't actually LOOKED at the sky
in months. The alarm has been ringing your whole life. You finally hear it.

Avicii "Wake Me Up" energy — folk/acoustic warmth crashing into euphoric EDM.
The message: you don't need permission to wake up. You don't need a movement.
You just need to open your eyes. Right now. This second.
