# Burning Through

## Concept
The physical wake-up call. Every other song on the album is cerebral —
algorithms, systems, cages, sleepwalking through thoughts. This one is
about the BODY. The moment you stop thinking and start feeling.

Not about sex literally — about the fire of being alive. Running till
your lungs burn. Screaming at a concert till your voice breaks. Kissing
someone in the rain like an idiot. The raw, unoptimized, unfiltered
experience of being a human animal in a world that wants you to be
a well-behaved user.

You can't algorithm your way to feeling alive. Sometimes you just have
to burn through.

Kings of Leon rawness — sweaty, loud, unapologetic. The song you play
when you roll the windows down and stop caring.
