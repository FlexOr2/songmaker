# With a Little Help (2026)

## Concept
A reimagining of the Beatles classic for 2026. Not a cover — a new song
carrying the same spirit. In a world that tells you to be self-made,
self-optimized, self-sufficient — the most radical truth is: you can't
do this alone. And you were never supposed to.

The original was about friendship getting you through. This version is
about that same truth, but in a world where friendship has been replaced
by followers, community by content, and connection by Wi-Fi signal strength.
The answer to every problem on this album isn't a system, an app, or a
revolution — it's the person sitting next to you.

Warm, soulful, uplifting. Joe Cocker energy over a modern house beat.
The song you sing with your arm around a stranger at 3 AM.
