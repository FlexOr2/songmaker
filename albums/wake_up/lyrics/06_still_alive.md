# Still Alive

## Concept
The album closer. The celebration — but not a naive one. Everything IS broken.
The system IS rigged. The cage IS comfortable. But you walked outside anyway.
The sky is still there. Strangers still smile. Music still moves you. You're
not fixing the world tonight. You're just choosing to FEEL it again.

Dancing in the ruins. Joy as an act of resistance. Not because everything is
fine, but because you refused to go numb. The most radical thing you can do
in a world designed to sedate you is to be fully, painfully, beautifully alive.

CYRIL deep house — piano-driven, euphoric, clean. The kind of song that plays
at 2 AM when the whole club is one heartbeat.
