# Rat Race 2.0

## Concept

You did everything they told you. Studied hard, got the degree, took the job, paid the loans. And the finish line moved. Again. Rent goes up, wages don't, AI takes the jobs they told you to train for. Meanwhile billionaires tell you to "hustle harder" from their third yacht. The system isn't broken — it's working exactly as designed. You're not failing, you were set up. Darker, angrier than Comfortable Cage. Heavy orchestral rock with trip-hop undertones.
