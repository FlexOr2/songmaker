# Sleepwalking

## Concept

The quietest song on the album. Not angry, not protesting — just observing. Everyone's sleepwalking through life. Commute, work, scroll, sleep, repeat. Nobody remembers when they stopped dreaming. Not because they're stupid or lazy, but because the world made being numb the easiest option. The most dangerous thing isn't oppression — it's apathy. A whole generation too tired to care, too comfortable to change, too numb to feel. The song is a gentle wake-up call, not a scream — a whisper that says "you used to dream."
