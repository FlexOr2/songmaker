# Sorry (Not Sorry)

## Concept

The official apology track. Felix sent Tobi a birthday album with AI-generated vocals that sounded like dying robots. Tobi listened politely and said nothing. That silence was worse than any roast. This song acknowledges the crime and turns the shame into something smooth and honest.

**Core thesis**: I'm sorry the Monty Python one existed. But I'm not sorry I tried.
