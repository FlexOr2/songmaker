## Song Concept

The official apology track. Felix sent Tobi a birthday album (his 41st) with AI-generated vocals that sounded like dying robots. Tobi — who's worked at Spotify, Sony Music, has been a DJ, is a natural-born singer and entertainer — listened politely and said nothing. That silence was worse than any roast. This song acknowledges the crime, names the specific terrible songs, and turns the shame into something smooth and honest.

**Core thesis**: I'm sorry the Monty Python one existed. But I'm not sorry I tried.
