## Song Concept

A birthday song from Flex0r to MC Tobbisch — part celebration, part apology, part love letter to a friendship that doesn't need daily contact to be real. The joke running through it: Felix built an AI music engine and the first person he's sending songs to is an actual music industry professional. The heart of it: distance doesn't kill real friendships, and some people just deserve a song on their birthday.

**Core thesis**: You're the real one. This song is fake. But the feeling is not.

**Tone**: Warm, funny, celebratory. Not cheesy — honest.

## Production Notes

### Style Reference
- Mark Ronson "Uptown Funk" energy but less bombastic
- Bruno Mars "24K Magic" groove
- German touch: Denglisch flow, real talk bridge

### Key Design Decisions
- **Denglisch**: Matches how Felix and Tobi actually talk — German base with English sprinkled in
- **Self-aware humor**: The song openly jokes about being AI-generated. That honesty IS the charm
- **"Das letzte Album war Müll"**: Direct apology for the Bark-era songs. Turns shame into comedy
- **"GG WP" outro**: Gaming callback that ties to the Download Days / Fire in the Hole universe
- **No fake depth**: This isn't pretending to be poetry. It's a friend saying happy birthday with the tools he has
- **Music industry references**: Spotify, Sony, DJ — acknowledges Tobi's actual career, shows respect
- **"Freunde seit Tag eins"**: The emotional payoff. Simple. True.
