# Happy Birthday Tobbisch

## Concept

A birthday song from Flex0r to MC Tobbisch — part celebration, part apology, part love letter to a friendship that doesn't need daily contact to be real. The joke running through it: Felix built an AI music engine and the first person he's sending songs to is an actual music industry professional. The heart of it: distance doesn't kill real friendships, and some people just deserve a song on their birthday.

**Core thesis**: You're the real one. This song is fake. But the feeling is not.

**Tone**: Warm, funny, celebratory. Not cheesy — honest.
