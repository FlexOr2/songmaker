## Song Concept

The meta song. Felix can't sing, can't rap, can't play an instrument. So he taught a machine to do it. This song is about that absurd journey — the 4 AM coding sessions, the terrible first attempts with Bark, the prompts that went wrong, the neighbors who think he's insane, and the stubborn refusal to give up. It's proud, funny, and completely self-aware.

**Core thesis**: Ich kann nichts davon, aber ich hab's trotzdem gemacht. Fuer dich.

**Tone**: Upbeat, funny, proud. Like a victory lap for a race you came in last.
