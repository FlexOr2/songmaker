# Friday at Murphy's

## Concept

A Beatles-style storytelling song — think "Penny Lane" meets "Eleanor Rigby" meets "Ob-La-Di, Ob-La-Da." Each verse introduces a character at Murphy's pub like a little vignette. Bright, melodic, clever wordplay. The kind of song that sounds cheerful but has layers if you listen closely. Jangly guitars, piano fills, harmonies on the chorus.
