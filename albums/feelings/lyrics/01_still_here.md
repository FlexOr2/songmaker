# Still Here

## Concept

A song about the people who stay. Not the ones who promise forever and leave, but the ones who never promise anything and are just... still there. Twenty years later. Still picking up the phone. Still laughing at the same jokes. The ones who don't need to say "I love you" because they show it by never disappearing.
