# Läuft

## Concept

The opening track. Felix's daily routine from the outside looks like a guy who has his shit together — software engineer, gym every day, functional adult. But from the inside it's autopilot. Wake up hungover, rush to the meeting, isolate in home office, train for hours with earphones so nobody talks to you, come home, 3-4 beers, YouTube nonsense, fall asleep drunk, wake up at 3am, turn off the screen, repeat. "Läuft" is what you say when you don't want to answer the question.
