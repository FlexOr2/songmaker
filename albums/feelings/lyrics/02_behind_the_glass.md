# Hinter Glas

## Concept

Felix saw Sabrina in religion class at 14. Half Turkish, black hair, a laugh that changed everything. They got together in 11th grade, she lived in Röttenbach near Erlangen. She went to study in Regensburg — weekend relationship. She always came to Erlangen. He almost never visited her. He was gaming CS, started poker, had no plan for his life, just studying because everyone did. She led the relationship — decided what they did, he went along. First holiday together in Alanya, Turkey. Seven years together until 24. He drifted, she grew. She cheated, he forgave, but she said "wir haben uns ausgelebt." Now 41, gym every day, drinks alone. All friends married with kids. Doesn't let anyone in.
